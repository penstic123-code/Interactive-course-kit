# Skin fieldbook: field notebook

Purpose: this skin's style, variables, illustration style and what it suits. This is the English translation of the Chinese README, which remains the source of truth.

**In one line**: on a kraft-paper desk lies an open page of grid loose-leaf paper, with binder holes and a red margin line on the left and coloured part-index tabs on the right. What the researcher writes goes straight onto the paper (a coloured-pencil vertical rule + highlighter subheading); things pasted into the notebook (photos, illustrations, diagrams, interaction boards, index cards) are pieces of paper with the same texture, fixed with tape.

Part main colours `--p1…p6`, each `a/b/d`: ochre / teal / olive / terracotta / indigo / plum for six parts (from part 7 on, back to part 1). Chapters in the same part only shift the coloured-pencil line's hue slightly (`--vl-dh × 0.6`); the writing colour stays. Paper grain `body[data-pat]`: 0 grid, 1 ruled, 2 dots, 3 plain.

## What is what

| Pencil layer: written straight on the paper (no ground, frame or shadow) | Paper layer: things pasted in (1px paper edge + light shadow + 3px radius + matte grain) |
|---|---|
| Learning goals, misconceptions, key points, one-line summary: a coloured-pencil vertical rule + highlighter subheading; Try it: a slightly tilted to-do box, ticked when done; timeline: dashed line + dots | **Heaviest**: interaction boards (graph paper, a part-colour line along the top, `--shadow-obj`), numbered figures and diagrams (white paper + tape). **Medium**: concept cards (index cards, with a red header line and a picture slot taped on crooked), photos (white-edged photo paper, old-photo tone, blue-green tape, tilted 0.8°), quiz cards, example carousel (loose-leaf divider tabs + one sheet). **Light**: why-it-works box (kraft archive page + archive tag, lying flat with no shadow), collapsed blocks (folded notes), guess-then-reveal (yellow sticky note) |

Controls are made of paper: warm paper ground, 1.5px ink-line edge, 4px radius, no cold blue-grey. Besides the kit's `file news diary letter lab`, document props include `ticket` (ticket stub), `clipping` (newspaper clipping) and `copy` (photocopy).

## Illustration style (the drawing kit in `skin.js`, with the kit's interface)

- Ink line + pale colour: people have clothing colour blocks, skin-coloured hands and a short neck; **two dots for eyes and one line for the mouth** (`o / shout / wide` are small round mouths, `sad` a slight curve, `closed` two short lines); faces sit in a `.norough` group and do not wobble.
- Coordinates follow the kit: `person(x, y)` has y at the head centre; standing feet at `y+64·s`, sitting at `y+63·s`, landing exactly on `ground()` (126); `legs:"sit"` brings its own wooden stool (`chair:false` turns it off).
- Outfit changes: within one picture, in turn none → baseball cap → long hair → bucket hat → straw hat, with the top's colour rotating at the same time. The kit's hat names map as `beanie`→bucket hat, `tophat / crown / wizard`→pith helmet. Also `hair: short long bun bob curly bald`, `hat: bucket straw cap pith scarf`, `coat`, `bottom: skirt sarong`, `top`, `skin`.
- `arms`: all of the kit's poses (stand none up cover point hold wave chin throw club desk); the object in `holding()` is clamped between the two hands.
- Objects: the kit's book box cup phone die star coin, plus notebook letters basket bag shell trowel mic; scene pieces `stool table house tree sun`, and `P / Ci / E / B / W` (path, circle, ellipse, rectangle, pale colour block). All are on `VL.illus.kit`.
- **Coloured-pencil conversion**: Rough.js redraws the strokes inside `.vl-il-rough` groups as pencil lines, and turns large colour areas into coloured-pencil hatching on a pale watercolour wash. An illustration is processed only when it enters the viewport (hidden carousel pages and collapsed sections can't be measured); colour areas under text get only the pale wash with no hatching. Without rough.js it falls back to the slight wobble of `#vl-fb-wobble`. For illustrations drawn after the page scripts, call `VL_SKIN.roughen(rootNode)`. Add `data-nohatch` to a picture that should be outlined only, without hatching.
- Diagrams (diagrams.js) are not converted to coloured pencil, just given the slight wobble of `--dg-filter`; data charts stay straight.
- One drawing style per page: in chapters using this skin, interactions and illustrations must also look like hand-drawn paper, with no photorealistic materials.

## Own motion

No atmosphere animation. Only popovers sliding in lightly, the Try-it tick bouncing, and the quiz badge being stamped; `vl-fx-off` stops it all.

## When it fits

- Suits: humanities, social science, fieldwork, history, archaeology, languages, folklore; adults and high school students; courses with many photos, documents and tickets.
- Doesn't suit: courses with lots of precise data charts (usable, but the overall feel leans literary); young or gamified courses.

## Known issues

- Rough.js redraws each illustration the first time it enters the viewport, so on pages with many illustrations the first frame may stutter slightly while scrolling.
- Standing and sitting heights match the kit's stick figures but are wider; when moving over old illustrations written in the kit's coordinates, objects may need shifting a few pixels.
- "How it looks once extracted as a skin" has not been approved separately yet.

Body 13.9, secondary 8.7, notes 7.3 (7.7 on paper pieces), selected-button text on accent ≥11.5, each part's `--deep` 6.6–9.4, red pen 5.5, `--q*` 4.8–6.0, top bar text 10.9, focus 8.2.
